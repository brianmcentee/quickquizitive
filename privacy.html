Privacy policy

Effective October 27, 2026. This page describes QuickQuizitive v0.20.2 and its account service.

Information used

Google sign-in provides an account identifier, email address, and basic profile information such as your name and profile image. Supabase stores the authentication account and session records. QuickQuizitive stores your plan, monthly allowance, assignment identifiers, authorization state, and relevant timestamps to manage access and avoid charging twice for the same assignment in a month.

The extension stores its Supabase session tokens, basic account identifiers, pending run records, and cached release information in Chrome’s local extension storage. Local run records may include diagnostic messages. These tokens are used to authenticate requests and are not added to Norton’s page.

Question content

The runner reads question data and operates controls locally on the active Norton page. It does not send your Norton password, question text, or answers to QuickQuizitive’s backend or an external AI service. Norton continues to process activity and answer submissions through its own service.

Service providers

Google handles Google sign-in. Supabase provides authentication, the database, and API access. GitHub Pages hosts the download site and a public version file checked by the extension. These providers receive the requests necessary to operate their services, which may include technical information such as IP addresses and service logs. The website does not add advertising trackers or a separate analytics service.

Purpose and sharing

Account and usage information is used to provide sign-in, enforce the allowance, resume authorized assignments, and support operation of the service. QuickQuizitive does not sell account or coursework information. Service providers process information as needed to operate the service.

Retention and deletion

Account and assignment authorization records are retained until the account is deleted; older monthly records may remain after an allowance resets. Authentication and operational logs are also subject to the providers’ retention settings. To request account and usage-record deletion, email brianmcenteecool@gmail.com from the address associated with your account. We may need to verify ownership. Do not include passwords or coursework in your request.

Signing out removes the local sign-in session. Uninstalling removes the extension’s local data, but does not delete server-side account records. You can also manage or revoke QuickQuizitive’s Google connection through your Google Account settings.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact brianmcenteecool@gmail.com. Changes to this policy will be published here with an updated effective date.
